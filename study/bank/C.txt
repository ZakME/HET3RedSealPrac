Q: A cracked aluminum wheel is found during a pre-trip inspection. What is the correct repair?
+ Replace the wheel
- Weld the crack and paint it
- Braze the crack and test it
- Drill a stop hole at the end of the crack

Q: What is the result of a wheel bearing adjusted too tight?
+ The bearing overheats and fails early
- The wheel wobbles and the seal leaks
- The brakes pull to that side
- The tire wears on the outer shoulder
E: Too much preload creates heat and spalling. Too much end play lets the hub wobble, damaging seals and brakes.
