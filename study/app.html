Mock Red Seal exams

Each full mock is 135 four-option questions, split across the 9 sections and 41 tasks exactly like the official Red Seal exam breakdown (the biggest sections are D Electrical and G Hydraulics with 25 questions each). The draw also leans toward the real mix of question types: mostly procedure and critical thinking, only a few straight recall. There is no feedback until you submit, like the real thing. Mocks 1–5 are fixed sets, so a retake shows the same questions and you can see if you're improving. The pass mark is 70%.

Section exams

A fresh random exam for one section each time, in exam mode. Each one is about double that section's share of the real exam.
